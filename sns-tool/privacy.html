Privacy Policy – OUKA SNS Tool

Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. Who we are

Ouka Skill Training Center Pvt. Ltd. (Gaidakot, Nepal) and ABAS Co., Ltd. (Gifu, Japan). Contact: abas_group@outlook.jp

2. Information we access from TikTok

  • Basic profile information of the connected account: open ID, display name and profile picture (scope user.info.basic).
  • An access token and refresh token that allow the tool to upload videos to the connected account as drafts (scope video.upload).

We do not access followers, messages, comments, analytics or any data of other TikTok users.

3. How we use it

  • Display name and picture: only shown in the tool so our staff can confirm which account is connected.
  • Tokens: only used to upload videos made by our school to our own TikTok account as drafts.

4. Where it is stored

Tokens are stored only on our school's office computer in the operating system's secure keychain (macOS Keychain). They are not stored on any server, not written to files or logs, and not shared with anyone.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share TikTok data with any third party.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the tool is connected. You can disconnect at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions). When disconnected, we delete the stored tokens from the computer.
