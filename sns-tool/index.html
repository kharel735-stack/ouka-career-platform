OUKA SNS Tool

OUKA SNS Tool is a desktop application made by Ouka Skill Training Center (a Japanese language and skills training school in Gaidakot, Nepal). Our staff use it on the school's office computer to send the short educational videos we make to our own TikTok account.

Our videos explain life and work in Japan (rules, manners, language) for young people in Nepal who are preparing to study or work in Japan.

How it works

1
Connect TikTok (Login Kit)
A staff member clicks “Connect TikTok” and logs in with our school's TikTok account. The tool shows the connected account's display name and profile picture so the staff can confirm the right account is connected. (scope: user.info.basic)
2
Choose a video
The staff member chooses one of the videos our school made.
3
Send to TikTok (Content Posting API – Upload)
The tool uploads the video to the connected TikTok account's inbox as a draft. (scope: video.upload)
4
Finish in the TikTok app
The creator opens the TikTok notification, checks the video, writes the caption, chooses the privacy setting and posts it manually in the TikTok app. The tool never posts publicly by itself.

Who can use it

Only staff of Ouka Skill Training Center / ABAS Co., Ltd. The tool is used only with our own TikTok account and only with videos we created ourselves.
